Strip 2 · voice lines

Voices: 24 kHz → 48 kHz

Every hero line was rendered at 24 kHz, so nothing above 12 kHz exists: the air, the breath and the edge of consonants are missing. The remaster keeps each original voice exactly as cast below 10.5 kHz and adds the top octave regenerated by AudioSR, then de-esses, compresses gently and levels every line to −16 LUFS. A is the line in today's build, B the remaster. Open a spectrogram to see the top octave appear.

Strip 4 · measurement

Sound bank audit

Every clip in the Unity bank measured: loudness (max momentary, BS.1770), true peak, and where its spectrum ends. A clip whose spectrum stops dead below 15 kHz is a band-limited render and sounds dull next to a full-band one.
